Fixed Fares and Travel Flexibility All Year Round

With Flight Pass, enjoy fixed fares for your frequent travel between two cities throughout your subscription period, with no seasonal price increases. Choose the plan that suits your travel needs, whether you're traveling solo or with family and friends.

Sign up now call_made
  • done

    Travel Your Way

    • Choose from 8, 16, or 24 flights per year, based on your travel needs.
    • Select the payment option that suits you:
      • One-Time Payment (Annual): Pay once and receive your full annual flight credit immediately.
      • Quarterly Plan: Pay every three months and split your flight credits across four quarters throughout the year.
    • Use your flight credits to travel between the two cities selected in your subscription.
    • A one-way booking uses one flight credit, while a round trip uses two flight credits.
    • Book up to 3 days before departure.
    • Applicable taxes and fees are charged at the time of booking.
  • done

    Travel with Family and Friends

    Add family members and friends as beneficiaries to your plan and enjoy traveling together using your Flight Pass credits.
  • done

    Earn AlFursan Miles with Flight Pass

    AlFursan Miles are not automatically credited to your account when traveling with Flight Pass. To receive your eligible miles, submit a mileage credit request through Manage Booking on the Saudia website, through the Saudia app, or by contacting the Call Center.

    Subscribe, Book, and Manage Your Trips Easily

    Subscribe and book your flights easily through the Flight Pass website. You can manage your bookings through the Saudia website or the Saudia app.
